Core Concept:
	A longer term roguelike that is meant to provide a satisfying loop 
	while also allowing meaningful progress to be made in shorter time spans over multiple sessions.
	Each level is cave with many items hidden around, creatures with their own unique behaviors,
	and multiple paths through/exits that each have a different requirement to pass. 
	This game prioritizes a feeling of steady progress, a closed economy, familiarity, exploration, and coop.
	
Core features:
	- Procedural cave levels
	- Procedural entities
	- Non combat options
	- Procedural items
	- Hidden items
	- Destructible terrain
	- Drop in coop
	- Complex and deep character AI
	- Unique characters
